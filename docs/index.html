Not All Errors Cost the Same

Bayesian Foundations of Evaluation Metrics for Fraud Detection — from F1 to Precision@Recall

Article in progress — Phase 0

A technical case interview revealed that F1 had been chosen on autopilot — without consciously connecting it to business cost, error asymmetry, or threshold choice. This article is the study that should have happened before that interview.

The central argument: Precision and Recall are conditional probabilities. Their relationship is governed by Bayes' theorem. Class imbalance is the base rate problem in disguise. F1 treats all errors as equally costly — Precision@recall does not. In fraud detection, the difference matters.
